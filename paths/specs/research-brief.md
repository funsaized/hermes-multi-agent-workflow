# Deliverable spec — research brief

Produce `brief.md` and `source-ledger.md`.

`brief.md` must contain: executive takeaway; Skills Map placement; behavioral
learner profile; current-to-target capability transition; minimal dependency map;
why the skill matters; current engineering practice; worked example; tradeoffs and
failure modes; decision checklist; short active self-check; open questions; and
dated sources. Include a fast path for readers who already demonstrate a
prerequisite. Every material claim must map to `source-ledger.md`.
